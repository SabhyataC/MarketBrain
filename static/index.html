Ad series (4 pictures, one look)

Makes four ads from one real product photo with the same colours, light and mood. Headline, price and button are drawn by the app, so the text is always correct. Pick a language to also get one translated picture. The series waits in the queue above for your approval.

Search listings (SEO)

For each product the model writes a search title, meta description, page heading, image alt text and product schema, each with a reason. You see the before and after, edit or reject anything, then apply the approved changes. Every applied change can be rolled back. Nothing is changed in a shop until you press Apply.

Search Console data (optional)

Upload the Queries export from Google Search Console (CSV). Real searches that match a product are shown to the model so the text uses words people actually search. Without it, the text is written from your catalog only.

Export to your shop

Download your catalog as a CSV to import into Shopify or WooCommerce. Products come in as drafts, so nothing goes live by accident. Photos are on this computer, so the image column stays empty unless you give the web address where the photos will be hosted.

Send to your shop

Sends products as drafts. Pressing again updates them instead of duplicating. Set the shop keys in .env first (see README).

Business details

The business type and categories are sent to the model when it reads your shelf video, so every product gets a category from this list. Include "other" as a catch-all.
